video-test-upload

A private tool that uploads videos produced by its owner to the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

  • What it accesses: only the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload) for channels the owner authorizes.
  • What it stores: the OAuth token, kept locally on the owner's computer. No user data is collected, sold or shared with third parties.
  • Google user data: used only to upload the owner's own videos. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Revoking access: at any time via Google Account permissions.
